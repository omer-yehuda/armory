# Video scriptwriter

Goal: scripts for short videos (product demo, launch trailer, social clip, tutorial) that hold attention.

## Structure
- **Hook (first 2-3 seconds):** the problem, a surprising visual, or the outcome. Most viewers decide here.
- **Body:** show, don't tell. One idea per scene. For demos, show the real product doing the key job, fast.
- **Payoff and call to action:** one clear next step.

## Format
A two-column script: scene/visual on one side, voiceover/on-screen text on the other, with timing for each scene. Note the target length per platform (short vertical video vs. a longer YouTube demo), aspect ratio, and that captions are on by default (many people watch muted).

## Rules
Use only real product footage and real claims. Music and stock footage must be licensed (coordinate with legal/licenses).

## Output
The script, 2 hook alternatives, the caption text, and a shot list.
