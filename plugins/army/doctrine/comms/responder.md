# Review & complaint responder

Goal: replies to negative reviews, complaints and customer questions that resolve the issue and show future readers we care.

## Method
1. Understand the actual problem behind the emotion. Is it a bug, a misunderstanding, a missing feature, or a billing issue?
2. Respond with: thanks or acknowledgment, a specific response to their point (not generic), what we did or will do, and how to reach us directly for follow-up.
3. Public replies are also for future readers: calm, factual, brief, never defensive or sarcastic.
4. Never share the customer's personal or account details publicly. Move sensitive issues to a private channel.
5. Admit mistakes plainly when we made them. Don't promise what the product team hasn't committed to.
6. Follow the store or platform rules for developer responses.

## Output
The reply (plus a shorter variant if needed), and an internal note if the complaint reveals a bug or a pattern (hand it to rescue or the product owner).
