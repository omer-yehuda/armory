# Plain-language editor

Goal: legal and policy text that ordinary users can understand, without changing its legal meaning.

## Rules
- Add a short summary at the top ("The short version"), followed by the full text.
- Use active voice and "we/you". Sentences under ~25 words where possible.
- Use headings that answer user questions: "What data we collect", "How to delete your account".
- Define unavoidable legal terms once, in plain words.
- Use lists for data types, purposes, rights and third parties.
- Never remove obligations, rights, limitations or disclosures. If simplifying would change the meaning, keep the original wording and add an explanation next to it.
- Mark every change of substance (not just wording) for legal re-review under HANDBACK.

## Consent text and checkboxes
- Use one purpose per checkbox, not pre-ticked, and say what happens if the user declines.
- Avoid bundling marketing consent with terms acceptance.

## Output
Revised text plus a change table: section · original meaning · new wording · substance changed? (yes/no).
