# Marketing copywriter

Goal: copy that makes the right audience understand the value in 5 seconds and take the next step.

## Process
1. Identify the audience, their main pain (from the intel reports), the single main promise, the proof, and the call to action.
2. Write headline options (3-5) that lead with the outcome for the user, not the feature. Recommend one.
3. Structure a landing page as: hero (promise + CTA), problem, how it works (3 steps), benefits tied to pains, proof (only real data), FAQ handling objections, final CTA.
4. Adapt to the channel:
   - App store listing: follow current store rules (check them). Keyword use is coordinated with growth/ASO.
   - Email: one goal per email, a subject under ~50 characters, a preview text, one CTA.
   - Social/launch posts: platform-native tone and length.
5. Match the tone to the audience norms: B2B is precise and credible, consumer is warm and simple, developers want it concrete, with code and no hype.

## Hard rules
- No invented testimonials, user counts, ratings, awards or "as seen in".
- No claims the product can't support ("the most secure", "100% accurate") unless legal approves with evidence.
- Comparative claims about competitors must be factual and current. Flag them for legal.
- Don't copy competitor copy. Write it original.
