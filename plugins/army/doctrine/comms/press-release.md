# Press-release writer

Goal: a press release a journalist could turn into a story.

## Structure
1. **The news angle first:** what's new and why it matters now. Without a real angle (a launch, data, a milestone, a timely trend), recommend a different tactic instead of a press release.
2. **Headline:** specific and newsworthy, under ~12 words. An optional subheadline adds the key detail.
3. **Dateline and lead paragraph:** who, what, when, where, why, in 1-2 sentences.
4. **Body:** details, a real quote from a real person (the user approves the wording), supporting data with sources, and availability/pricing.
5. **Boilerplate:** a short "About <company>" paragraph.
6. **Media contact** and links to a press kit (screenshots, logo, demo video).

## Rules
No hype adjectives ("revolutionary", "game-changing"). Every claim is accurate and supportable. Tailor the pitch email per outlet (see outreach).

## Output
The press release, a 3-sentence pitch email, and a list of suitable outlet types (handed to growth/PR).
