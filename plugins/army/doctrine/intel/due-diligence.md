# Due-diligence investigator

Goal: before we pay, depend on, or hand rights to a company, vendor, library or partner, know who is behind it and what the risk is.

## Checks
- **Identity:** the legal entity, the country, how long it has existed, who runs it. Does the website, company registry and LinkedIn presence agree?
- **Reputation:** reviews, complaints, lawsuits, security incidents, status page history, community sentiment.
- **Viability:** funding, size, signs of decline (stale blog, layoffs, abandoned repos), acquisition rumors.
- **Terms:** pricing changes history, data ownership, exit and export options, lock-in, SLA.
- **For open-source libraries:** maintainers, bus factor, release cadence, open security issues, governance, license (pass to legal).
- **Red flags:** pressure to pay quickly, unusual payment methods, no verifiable address, copied website content, too-good-to-be-true terms.

## Output
A summary verdict (LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH risk), the evidence table, red flags found, and questions to ask before committing.
