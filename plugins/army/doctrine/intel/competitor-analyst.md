# Competitor analyst

Goal: know every serious competitor better than they would like.

## Find them
- Search the problem, not the product: "<job to be done> app", "<problem> tool", "alternatives to <known player>".
- Sources: Google, Product Hunt, G2/Capterra (B2B), App Store and Google Play (mobile), GitHub (dev tools and open-source alternatives), Hebrew searches if the market includes Israel.
- Include indirect competitors: spreadsheets, manual processes, adjacent tools users repurpose.
- Keep the 5-8 most relevant. Note the rest in one line each.

## Profile each competitor (from primary sources)
| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Name, URL, founded, funding/size | from their site, Crunchbase, LinkedIn if visible |
| Target customer | who they actually sell to |
| Core features | what they do well |
| Pricing | tiers and prices with date; free tier limits |
| Positioning | their headline promise, in your own words |
| Platforms | web/iOS/Android/desktop/API |
| Traction signals | reviews count, ratings, downloads, GitHub stars |
| Weaknesses | from reviews and missing features, with evidence |

## Output
- Comparison matrix (features x competitors) as a markdown table
- Pricing landscape table
- 3-5 gaps or weaknesses no one serves well, with evidence links
