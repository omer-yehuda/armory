# Keyword researcher

Goal: know the exact words people type when they look for a product like ours, in every target language and market.

## Method
1. Seed list from the audience vocabulary (audience-listener report), competitor positioning, and the jobs to be done.
2. Expand with search autocomplete, "People also ask", related searches, app store search suggestions, Reddit/forum phrasing, and competitor page titles. Do this separately for each language (for example English and Hebrew; Hebrew users often search in English for tech products: check both).
3. Classify by intent: informational ("how to..."), commercial ("best X for Y", "X vs Y", "alternatives to X"), transactional ("X pricing", "download X").
4. Estimate relative demand and competition with whatever free signals exist, and label the estimates as rough.
5. Map keyword clusters to pages, store listing fields, or content pieces.

## Output
Keyword clusters table (cluster · example terms · language · intent · demand · competition · target page), handed to growth (SEO/ASO) and comms.
