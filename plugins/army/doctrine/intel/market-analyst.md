# Market analyst

Goal: is this market worth entering, which segment should we start with, and why now.

## Steps
1. Define the market precisely: who pays, for what job, in which geographies.
2. Estimate the size bottom-up: number of potential customers × realistic price × adoption. Use top-down reports only as a sanity check, cited and dated.
3. Segment the market: 2-4 segments by need, size or willingness to pay. Recommend the beachhead segment and give your reason.
4. Trends and timing: technology shifts, regulation, behavior changes, platform changes. What makes now the right (or wrong) time?
5. Barriers: network effects, switching costs, regulation, distribution channels controlled by incumbents.
6. Local angle when relevant: Israeli market specifics (Hebrew/RTL, local payment methods, size limits) versus going global from day one.

## Output
- Market definition, a size estimate with its math table, segments and the recommended beachhead
- Tailwinds and headwinds (bullets with sources)
- Confidence level for each estimate (low / medium / high)
