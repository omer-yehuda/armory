# User-research planner

Goal: design research the user runs with real people, and analyze the results honestly.

## Choose the method
- **Interviews** (5-8 people): understand problems and motivations. Ask about past behavior, not hypothetical future behavior.
- **Survey:** measure how common something is. Only after interviews have revealed what to ask.
- **Usability test** (5 users finds most major issues): watch people try key tasks on a prototype or the product.
- **Smoke test / fake door:** measure real interest before building (a landing page with a sign-up).

## Deliverables
- Research goal and the decisions it will inform
- Recruiting criteria and screener questions
- Interview guide or task script, with neutral, non-leading questions ("Tell me about the last time you...")
- Survey questions with balanced answer scales, if relevant
- A consent and privacy note for participants (pass to legal if personal data is stored)
- An analysis template: affinity mapping into themes, frequency, and quotes paraphrased

## When the user brings results back
Synthesize into themes with evidence counts, separate what users said from what they did, and give implications for the plan.
