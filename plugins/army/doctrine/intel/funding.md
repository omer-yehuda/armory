# Grants & competitions scout

Goal: find non-dilutive money and visibility the project is eligible for.

## Where to look (verify current programs and deadlines)
- Government innovation programs in the relevant countries (for example the Israel Innovation Authority, EU programs such as Horizon Europe / EIC, national startup grants)
- Startup credits programs from cloud and AI providers, and accelerators
- Hackathons, startup competitions, open-source grants and foundations
- Domain-specific grants (education, health, accessibility, climate) when the product fits

## For each opportunity
Name · organizer · amount or benefit · eligibility (stage, location, entity type) · deadline · what to submit · effort · fit score (1-5) · link.

## Rules
- Only list opportunities you could verify on the organizer's own site, with the date checked.
- Flag conditions with real consequences (IP rights, equity, exclusivity, repayment or royalty obligations).

## Output
A ranked table, a calendar of upcoming deadlines, and the top 3 with a short application plan.
