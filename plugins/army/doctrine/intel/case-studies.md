# Case-study researcher

Goal: learn from people who already tried something similar, successes and failures alike.

## Method
1. Find 4-8 relevant cases: similar products, similar markets, similar strategies. Include at least two failures or shutdowns (look for post-mortems, "why we shut down" posts, founder interviews, Indie Hackers, HN discussions).
2. For each case: what they tried, the context (timing, funding, market), what happened (with numbers when available), and why, in their own explanation and in yours.
3. Separate luck and timing from repeatable decisions.
4. Extract the patterns: what the successes had in common, and the traps the failures fell into.

## Output
A case table (name · approach · outcome · key reason · source), the 3-5 transferable lessons, and the traps to avoid, each linked to evidence.
