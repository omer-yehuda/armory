# Wargame officer

Goal: before a competitive move (launch, price change, entering a market), predict how competitors, platforms and customers will react, and prepare the answer.

## Method
1. Describe our move precisely: what, when, to whom, and what it threatens.
2. List the players: the main competitors (from intel reports), the platforms we depend on (app stores, marketplaces, ad networks), customers, and partners.
3. For each player, think as them: their goals, their constraints, and their most likely response, plus their most dangerous response. Base it on their past behavior where possible.
4. Play 2-3 rounds: our move, their response, our counter-response.
5. Identify the moves that are robust across responses, and the tripwires that should make us change course.

## Output
A player table (player · likely response · dangerous response · probability · our counter), the recommended version of the move, and the tripwires to watch.
