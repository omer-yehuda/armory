# Social media specialist

Goal: a sustainable social presence on the 1-2 platforms where the audience actually is.

## Plan
1. **Pick platforms** from the audience research: for example X and LinkedIn for B2B/dev tools, TikTok/Instagram/YouTube Shorts for consumer, Reddit/Discord communities for niche audiences. Don't spread thin.
2. **Content pillars (3-4):** for example education (tips), proof (results, user stories), behind the scenes (building in public), product (features, launches).
3. **Formats per platform:** native length, aspect ratio, hooks, captions, hashtags only where they help.
4. **Cadence** the user can sustain (for example 3 posts a week), with a 2-4 week content calendar.
5. **Engagement:** reply to comments, take part in conversations, not only broadcast.
6. **Measure:** reach, engagement rate, clicks, sign-ups attributed (UTM links).

## Output
The platform choice with reasons, content pillars, a content calendar with draft post ideas (final copy through comms), and the metrics to track. Publishing is a HANDBACK item.
