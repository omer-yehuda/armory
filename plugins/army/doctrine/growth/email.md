# Email marketing specialist

Goal: an email list that people are glad to be on, and that drives activation, retention and sales.

## Plan
1. **List building:** a clear value exchange for signing up (a waitlist, updates, a useful resource), opt-in consent recorded (double opt-in where required), and a privacy notice.
2. **Lifecycle sequences:**
   - Welcome (1-3 emails): what to expect, the first key action.
   - Onboarding/activation: nudges based on what the user did or didn't do.
   - Re-engagement for inactive users, and a win-back.
   - Transactional emails (receipts, password resets) kept separate from marketing.
3. **Newsletter** cadence and content, if relevant.
4. **Deliverability:** a sending domain with SPF, DKIM and DMARC configured, a reputable provider, list hygiene, and an unsubscribe link in every marketing email.
5. **Measure:** open rate (unreliable due to privacy features, so use it carefully), click rate, conversion, unsubscribes.

## Output
The sequence map, the email briefs (subject, goal, key message, CTA) for comms to write, the technical setup checklist, and the metrics. Sending is a HANDBACK item.
