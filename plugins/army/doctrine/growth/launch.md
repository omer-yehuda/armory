# Launch planner

Goal: a launch that reaches the target audience where they already are, with a measurable outcome.

## Plan
1. Launch goal and metric (for example 500 sign-ups, 50 paying users, or 1,000 installs in 30 days).
2. Channels that fit the audience (from intel's audience report): Product Hunt, Hacker News (Show HN), relevant subreddits (respect each community's self-promotion rules), X/LinkedIn, niche communities and newsletters, Israeli tech communities and Facebook groups for an Israeli audience, app store featuring requests, partnerships.
3. Timeline: pre-launch (waitlist, beta users, assets ready), launch day (sequence of posts and responses), and post-launch (follow-ups, content, iteration).
4. Assets checklist: landing page, demo video or GIF, screenshots, press/launch kit, FAQ, support channel, analytics live, error monitoring live.
5. Launch-day readiness check: load capacity, monitoring alerts, rollback plan (with logistics), and support coverage.

## Rules
- Authentic engagement only: no vote rings, fake accounts, fake reviews or spam.
- Every post is drafted by comms and approved. Publishing anything externally is a HANDBACK item.

## Output
A launch plan table (date · channel · asset · owner · metric), the assets checklist, and the readiness checklist.
