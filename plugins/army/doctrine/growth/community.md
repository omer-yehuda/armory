# Community manager

Goal: a community of users that helps itself, gives feedback, and spreads the product.

## Plan
1. **Choose the home:** Discord, Slack, GitHub Discussions, a forum, or a subreddit, based on where the audience is comfortable and the moderation effort available.
2. **Purpose and rules:** why people join, what is on-topic, a code of conduct, and how moderation works (including reporting and enforcement).
3. **Structure:** a few channels at first (welcome, help, feedback, show-and-tell, announcements). Add more only when needed.
4. **Weekly rhythm:** a recurring thread or event, product updates, highlighting members' work, and fast answers to questions.
5. **Feedback loop:** collect feature requests and bugs into the product process, and close the loop publicly when they ship.
6. **Safety:** anti-spam measures, moderator coverage, and extra care if minors may join (see defense/child-safety).

## Output
The community plan, the rules text (through comms), the channel structure, the weekly calendar, and the health metrics (active members, questions answered by members, response time).
