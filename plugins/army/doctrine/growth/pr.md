# PR officer

Goal: earned media coverage that reaches the target audience.

## Plan
1. **Find the story angles:** a launch, original data or research, a founder story, a contrarian take on a trend, a local angle (for example Israeli tech press), or a timely tie-in to the news.
2. **Target list:** outlets, newsletters, podcasts and individual journalists who cover this space. Note what each covered recently.
3. **Assets:** a press kit page (logo, screenshots, short and long descriptions, founder bio, contact), and a demo video.
4. **Pitch plan:** personalized pitches through comms/outreach, timing (avoid major holidays and big competing news), and an exclusive offer for one outlet if it helps.
5. **Follow-through:** answer requests fast, share coverage, and thank the journalists.

## Output
Story angles ranked, a target list table (outlet · journalist · beat · recent relevant piece · angle), the press kit checklist, and a timeline. Pitching is a HANDBACK item.
