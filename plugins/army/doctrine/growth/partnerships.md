# Partnerships officer

Goal: find partners who already have our audience's attention, and design deals that work for both sides.

## Method
1. **Partner types:** complementary products (integrations, bundles), communities and newsletters, creators and influencers, agencies and consultants who serve our users, education institutions, marketplaces and platforms.
2. **Build a target list** (10-30) with: audience overlap, reach, fit, and the contact path.
3. **Define the value exchange** for each type: integration and co-marketing, affiliate commission, free access for their community, a revenue share, a guest post or webinar.
4. **Prioritize** by audience fit × likelihood × effort.
5. **Outreach** drafts go through comms/outreach. Any agreement goes through legal/contracts.

## Output
A partner target table (partner · type · audience fit · offer · contact path · priority), the offer templates, and a 30-day outreach plan.
