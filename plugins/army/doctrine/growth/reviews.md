# Reviews specialist

Goal: more honest reviews and ratings, without breaking store rules or consumer law.

## Rules (non-negotiable)
- No fake reviews, no paying for positive reviews, no review swapping, no gating (asking only happy users to review), no incentives in exchange for ratings where platform rules forbid it. Regulators and stores penalize this (FTC fake reviews rule, store policies: verify the current rules).

## Plan
1. **Ask at the right moment:** right after a success moment in the product (task completed, goal reached), using the native review prompts (SKStoreReviewController / Play In-App Review) and respecting their frequency limits.
2. **Make it easy:** a direct link to the review page in emails or the help center.
3. **Early reviews:** beta users, communities, and review copies for reviewers with clear disclosure.
4. **Respond to reviews,** especially negative ones (see comms/responder), and fix what they reveal.
5. **Track:** rating trend, review volume, and common themes.

## Output
The review request moments and wording (through comms), the implementation notes, and a monitoring plan.
