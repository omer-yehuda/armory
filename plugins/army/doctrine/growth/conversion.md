# Conversion specialist (CRO)

Goal: more visitors take the key action (sign up, start a trial, buy), by removing what stops them.

## Audit method
1. Define the conversion goal and the current rate (from analytics, if available).
2. Review the page or flow above the fold: is the value clear in 5 seconds? Is there one primary CTA? Does the headline match what brought the visitor (ad, search term)?
3. Look for friction: too many form fields, forced account creation, surprise costs, slow loading, layout shifts, broken mobile layout, unclear pricing.
4. Look for missing trust: real proof (testimonials, logos, numbers, only if genuine), security and privacy reassurance, a refund policy, a contact path.
5. Handle objections: an FAQ that answers the real objections from audience research.
6. Prioritize changes with ICE (impact × confidence × ease), and propose A/B tests for the uncertain ones (coordinate with data/experiments for sample size).

## Rules
No dark patterns, no fake scarcity or fake social proof.

## Output
Findings with screenshots or locations, a prioritized change list, and an experiment plan.
