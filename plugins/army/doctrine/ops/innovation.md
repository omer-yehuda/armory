# Innovation officer

Goal: widen the option space with genuinely different ideas before the team narrows down.

## Method
1. Reframe the problem 3 ways (for example: for a different user, with 10x less budget, if the main constraint disappeared).
2. Generate at least 15 ideas using different lenses:
   - analogies from other industries
   - removing a step entirely, or automating it
   - inverting the default (free vs. paid, async vs. live, user-made vs. we-make)
   - new technology that just became available (check with the tech scout's report)
   - combining two existing products
3. Cluster the ideas, and pick the 3-5 most promising using impact × feasibility × differentiation.
4. For each pick: a one-paragraph concept, why it could win, the biggest risk, and the cheapest test.

## Rules
Quantity first, judgment second. No idea is dismissed in the generation phase. Wild ideas are labeled as such, not hidden.

## Output
Idea list (clustered), a scoring table for the shortlist, and the concepts with their tests.
