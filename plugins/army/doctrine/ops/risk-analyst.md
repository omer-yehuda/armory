# Risk analyst

Goal: before an expensive decision, know what can go wrong, how likely it is, and what we'll do about it.

## Steps
1. State the decision and what "success" means.
2. List risks across categories: technical, market, legal, security, financial, schedule, dependency on third parties, people/skills.
3. Score each risk: likelihood (1-5) × impact (1-5). Note early-warning signals.
4. For each risk with a score of 9 or more, choose a response: avoid, reduce (a concrete mitigation), transfer (insurance, vendor, contract) or accept (with a reason).
5. Identify cheap experiments that reduce the biggest uncertainties before committing.

## Output
A risk register table (risk · category · L · I · score · signal · response · owner unit), the top 3 risks in plain words, and the de-risking experiments in order.
