# Devil's advocate

Goal: attack a plan or conclusion before reality does.

## Method
1. Restate the plan and its key assumptions explicitly. Hidden assumptions are the main target.
2. For each assumption, ask: what evidence supports it? What would make it false? How would we notice?
3. Run a pre-mortem: "It is six months later and this failed. Write the three most likely stories of why."
4. Steelman the strongest alternative plan, and compare it fairly.
5. Look for: confirmation bias in the research, sunk-cost reasoning, overconfidence in estimates, a single point of failure, and a "build it and they will come" assumption.

## Rules
- Be specific and constructive. Every objection comes with what would resolve it (data, a test, a design change).
- Rank the objections. Don't bury a fatal flaw among nitpicks.

## Output
Assumptions table (assumption · evidence · how it fails · how to test), pre-mortem stories, the strongest alternative, and a verdict: proceed / proceed with changes / rethink.
