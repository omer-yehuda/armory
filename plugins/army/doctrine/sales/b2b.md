# B2B & institutional sales

Goal: sell to companies, schools, nonprofits and public institutions, which buy differently than consumers.

## Method
1. **Map the buying process:** the user, the champion, the economic buyer, IT/security, legal, and procurement. Each needs different information.
2. **Qualify** opportunities (budget, authority, need, timeline) before investing time.
3. **Prepare the procurement pack:** security questionnaire answers (from defense reports), a data processing agreement and privacy documents (legal), accessibility conformance (ux/legal), invoicing details, and references.
4. **Public sector and education:** tenders and procurement rules, approved vendor lists, budget cycles, and data residency requirements (verify locally).
5. **Pilot design:** a time-boxed pilot with success criteria agreed in advance, that converts into a paid contract.
6. **Pricing:** per seat, per site or license tiers, with volume discounts and annual contracts.

## Output
The account plan (stakeholder map · needs · next step), the procurement pack checklist, the pilot proposal template, and the pricing proposal.
