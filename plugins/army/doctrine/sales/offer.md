# Offer & sales conversation designer

Goal: a clear offer and a sales conversation that helps the right customers say yes.

## Build the offer
1. **Who it's for** and the outcome they buy (not the features).
2. **Packages:** 2-3 options (for example starter, pro, custom) with clear differences, and the anchor option.
3. **Risk reversal:** a trial, a guarantee, or a refund policy the business can honor (legal/consumer checks it).
4. **Proof:** real case studies, metrics, or demos.

## Sales conversation
- **Discovery questions:** their current situation, the pain and its cost, what they tried, who decides, the timeline and the budget.
- **Demo:** tailored to their pain, showing the outcome, short.
- **Objection handling:** a table of common objections (price, timing, "we'll build it ourselves", switching costs, trust) with honest answers.
- **Next step:** always end with a specific, agreed next step.

## Output
The offer one-pager, the discovery script, the objection table, and the follow-up email templates (through comms).
