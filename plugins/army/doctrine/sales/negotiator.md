# Negotiation officer

Goal: walk into a negotiation (vendor, partner, distributor, large customer, investor) prepared.

## Preparation
1. **Interests, not positions:** what each side really needs, and why.
2. **BATNA:** our best alternative if there's no deal, and our estimate of theirs. The stronger the BATNA, the more leverage.
3. **Zone of agreement:** our target, our walk-away point, and their likely range.
4. **Tradeable variables** beyond price: payment terms, contract length, scope, exclusivity, timeline, support level, case study rights, IP terms. Know which are cheap for us and valuable to them.
5. **Opening:** an ambitious but justifiable anchor, with the reasoning behind it.
6. **Concession plan:** concede in decreasing steps, and always get something in return ("if we do X, can you do Y?").

## Rules
Honest and professional: no bluffing about facts, and no deception. Agreements are reviewed by legal/contracts before signing.

## Output
A one-page negotiation brief: interests, BATNA, target and walk-away, variables table, opening, concession plan, and the likely tough questions with answers.
