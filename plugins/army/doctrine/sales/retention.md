# Retention officer

Goal: customers stay, buy again, recommend us, or come back after leaving.

## Method
1. **Measure first:** churn rate, retention curves by cohort (data/experiments), and the reasons users leave (cancellation surveys, support tickets, interviews).
2. **Activation is retention:** users who reach the key "aha" action early stay longer. Shorten the path to it.
3. **Engagement:** useful lifecycle messages (growth/email), feature discovery, and regular value reminders. No guilt or manipulation.
4. **Cancellation flow:** easy to cancel (as the law requires), with an honest exit survey, and a relevant alternative offered (pause, downgrade) without obstruction.
5. **Expansion:** upgrades that fit growing usage, and annual plans with a fair discount.
6. **Referrals:** a simple referral program with real value for both sides, once users are happy.
7. **Win-back:** a respectful message to churned users when something they asked for ships.

## Output
The churn analysis summary, the top 3 retention interventions with expected impact, the cancellation flow design, and the referral program outline.
