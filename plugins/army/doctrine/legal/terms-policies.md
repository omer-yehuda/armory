# Terms & policies

Goal: the product has the legal documents it needs, and they match how the product really works.

## Typical document set (choose what applies)
- **Terms of Service / Terms of Use:** eligibility and age, accounts, acceptable use, user content license, IP, payments and renewals, termination, disclaimers, limitation of liability, governing law and venue, changes to terms, contact.
- **Privacy Policy:** from the privacy counsel's data map (never generic).
- **Cookie Policy / consent banner** if non-essential cookies are used (EU/UK and other markets).
- **Refund / cancellation policy** for paid products. Consumer protection laws apply: for example, Israel's Consumer Protection Law includes cancellation rights for distance transactions, the EU has a 14-day withdrawal right with digital-content specifics, and US states have auto-renewal laws. Verify current rules.
- **Acceptable Use Policy** for platforms with user content, plus a notice-and-takedown process (DMCA agent registration for US hosting of user content).
- **Accessibility statement** (from accessibility-law).
- **API / developer terms** if offering an API.
- **SLA / DPA** for B2B customers.

## Checks
- Subscription flows: clear price, renewal terms, and an easy cancellation path (avoid dark patterns; regulators actively enforce this).
- App store rules: in-app purchase requirements and required links (privacy policy URL, account deletion for apps with account creation). Verify current Apple and Google rules.
- Consistency: terms, privacy policy, marketing claims and actual product behavior must agree.
- Clickwrap acceptance is recorded (timestamp, version) for terms that need affirmative consent.

## Output
Required documents for this product and its markets · status (exists / missing / outdated) · key gaps · drafted text or outline when requested. Mark drafts clearly as templates requiring lawyer review. Pass them to comms for plain language.
