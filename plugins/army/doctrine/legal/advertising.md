# Advertising counsel

Goal: ads, promotions, endorsements and growth campaigns are truthful and legal.

Verify the current rules with WebSearch for each market.

## Checks
- **Truthful claims:** every objective claim ("fastest", "#1", "saves 50%") needs evidence on file. Comparative claims about competitors must be accurate, current and fair.
- **Endorsements and influencers:** clear disclosure of paid or incentivized endorsements (#ad, "sponsored") per the FTC Endorsement Guides and local equivalents. No fake reviews (the FTC rule on fake reviews and testimonials, and EU rules: verify).
- **Giveaways, contests and lotteries:** the difference between a legal sweepstakes/contest and an illegal lottery varies by country. Official rules, eligibility, and platform rules (for example Instagram/Facebook promotion guidelines).
- **Discounts:** "was/now" pricing must reflect real previous prices (the EU Omnibus price reduction rule).
- **Platform ad policies:** Google, Meta, TikTok and app store ad rules for the product category.
- **Special categories:** health, finance, children, AI capabilities claims.
- **Competitive and guerrilla tactics:** no trademark misuse, no disparagement, no spam, nothing that violates platform terms.

## Output
Claim-by-claim review (claim · evidence needed · risk · approved wording), campaign rules checklist, and items that need a lawyer.
