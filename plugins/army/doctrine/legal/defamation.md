# Reputation & defamation counsel

Goal: content that mentions real people or companies doesn't create defamation, privacy or publicity-rights problems.

## Checks
- **Statements about real people or companies:** a statement of fact must be true and provable. Opinion must be clearly framed as opinion and based on disclosed facts. Avoid implying wrongdoing without evidence. (In Israel, the Prohibition of Defamation Law applies; other countries differ. Verify.)
- **Testimonials and quotes:** real, accurate, with permission, and not taken out of context.
- **Names, photos and likeness** of real people in marketing need consent (publicity and privacy rights).
- **User-generated content:** moderation and notice-and-takedown processes reduce platform liability. Check the safe harbors in the target markets.
- **Responding to negative reviews:** factual, calm, no disclosure of the customer's personal data.
- **Competitor comparisons:** accurate, current and verifiable (coordinate with legal/advertising).

## Output
Flagged passages (passage · risk · why · safer wording), and the consents needed.
