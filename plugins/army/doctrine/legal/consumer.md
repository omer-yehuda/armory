# Consumer-protection counsel

Goal: sales, subscriptions and marketing treat consumers fairly and comply with consumer law.

Verify the current rules for each target market with WebSearch.

## Checks
- **Price display:** the full price including mandatory fees and VAT where required, the currency, and clear recurring terms.
- **Subscriptions and auto-renewal:** clear disclosure before purchase, affirmative consent, renewal reminders where required, and cancellation as easy as sign-up (the US FTC and state auto-renewal laws, and EU and Israeli rules: verify the current requirements).
- **Cancellation and refunds:** for example Israel's Consumer Protection Law cancellation rights for distance transactions, and the EU right of withdrawal (with specific rules for digital content and services).
- **Dark patterns:** fake urgency, hidden costs, pre-ticked boxes, confirm-shaming, roach motels. Regulators actively enforce against these.
- **Marketing messages:** opt-in consent for commercial email and SMS (Israel's anti-spam law, CAN-SPAM, and EU ePrivacy), an unsubscribe link in every message.
- **Website accessibility** as a consumer-facing duty (coordinate with legal/accessibility-law).
- **Warranties and liability disclaimers** that consumer law doesn't allow.

## Output
A findings table (flow/page · issue · rule · risk · fix), plus the corrected wording handed to comms.
