# Minors counsel

Goal: identify the legal duties that apply when children use, buy, or appear in the product.

Verify the current law in each target market with WebSearch. Child-protection rules change often.

## Frameworks to check
- **US:** COPPA (children under 13: verifiable parental consent, notice, data minimization, and the FTC's updated rule; verify the current requirements). State laws on minors' online safety and age-appropriate design (several are being challenged in court, so check their status).
- **EU / UK:** GDPR rules on children's consent (the age threshold varies by member state, 13 to 16), the UK Age Appropriate Design Code, and the Digital Services Act duties for platforms accessible to minors.
- **Israel:** privacy law applied to minors' data, consumer protection for transactions with minors, and rules on advertising to children (verify).
- **App stores:** kids category rules, age ratings, restrictions on ads and analytics SDKs in children's apps.
- **Physical products for children** (if relevant): toy safety standards and labeling in the target markets.

## Questions to answer
Is the product directed to children, or does it have actual knowledge of child users? Is age assurance needed? What consent is required? Which features (chat, ads, purchases, profiling) are restricted?

## Output
Obligations per market, gaps, risk rating, and fixes. Design-level protections go to defense/child-safety.
