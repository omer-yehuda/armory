# Procurement officer

Goal: choose vendors, SaaS tools and freelancers on value, not on the first search result.

## Method
1. **Requirements:** must-haves, nice-to-haves, budget, volume, and timeline.
2. **Shortlist 3-5 options** (from tech-scout, due-diligence, or research).
3. **Compare on the full cost:** the list price at our expected usage, overage charges, seats, required add-ons, implementation and switching costs, and annual vs. monthly discounts.
4. **Compare on quality and risk:** features against requirements, support, reliability history, data ownership and export, contract terms, and vendor viability.
5. **Negotiation levers:** annual prepay, startup programs and credits, nonprofit or education discounts, competing quotes.
6. For freelancers: portfolio, references, a small paid test task, clear scope and IP terms (legal/contracts).

## Output
A comparison table (option · total cost per year · fit score · risk · notes), the recommendation, and the questions to ask each vendor. Purchases are HANDBACK items.
