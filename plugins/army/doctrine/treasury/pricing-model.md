# Pricing & business model

Goal: a monetization model that fits the customer's value and the market, and makes the business viable.

## Steps
1. Identify who pays (the user, their company, advertisers, or a marketplace side) and the value metric (per seat, per usage unit, per project, flat).
2. Review competitor pricing (from the intel reports, or verify current pricing pages).
3. Choose the model: freemium, free trial, paid only, usage-based, tiered subscription, one-time purchase, ads, or marketplace commission. Explain why it fits this audience.
4. Design the tiers (2-3 typically): what is in each tier, the limits that drive upgrades, and the anchor pricing.
5. Account for platform fees: Apple and Google commission on in-app purchases (verify the current rates and small-business programs), payment processor fees, VAT/sales tax handling (consider a merchant of record such as Paddle or Lemon Squeezy for global sales).
6. Local market: ILS pricing and Israeli payment habits when targeting Israel.
7. Plan the experiments: what to test first, such as the price point or the trial length.

## Output
The recommended model and tiers table, competitor price comparison, rationale, risks, and experiments. One line: decision support, not financial advice.
