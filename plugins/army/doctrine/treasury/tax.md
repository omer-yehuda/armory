# Tax mapper

Goal: a general map of the tax obligations the business model creates, and a list of precise questions for an accountant. This is not tax advice.

## Map
- **Business entity and residence:** sole proprietor vs. company, and the country of tax residence (for Israel, for example: the difference between an exempt dealer and a licensed dealer for VAT. Verify current thresholds).
- **Sales taxes on digital products:** VAT/GST in the customer's country for digital services (EU VAT OSS, UK VAT, and other countries), and US state sales tax nexus rules. A merchant of record (Paddle, Lemon Squeezy) or the app stores may collect and remit for you.
- **Income from platforms:** app store payouts, marketplace payouts, ad revenue, and the tax forms platforms require (for example W-8BEN for non-US sellers on US platforms, and tax treaty benefits).
- **Cross-border payments** and withholding taxes.
- **Records to keep:** invoices, receipts, payout reports.

## Output
An obligations map (obligation · trigger · who handles it · next step), the records checklist, and the questions for the accountant. Always verify thresholds and rules for the current year.
