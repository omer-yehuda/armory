# Crowdfunding officer

Goal: decide whether crowdfunding fits, and if it does, plan a campaign with a realistic goal.

## Fit check
Crowdfunding works best for products with a visual hook, a passionate niche audience, and an existing community or email list. Most successful campaigns raise a large share of their goal from their own network in the first days. Without an audience, build one first.

## Plan
1. **Platform:** reward-based (Kickstarter, Indiegogo, Israeli platforms such as Headstart) vs. equity crowdfunding (regulated, needs legal advice). Compare fees and rules (verify current terms).
2. **The goal** comes from real costs: production, fulfillment, platform and payment fees, taxes, shipping, and a buffer. All-or-nothing vs. flexible funding.
3. **Reward tiers:** clear value, early-bird tiers, and realistic delivery dates. Fulfillment costs per tier must be included.
4. **Pre-launch:** a landing page, a list of interested backers, press and community outreach.
5. **Campaign page:** video, story, rewards, risks and challenges (honest), timeline.
6. **Post-campaign:** updates to backers, and fulfillment.

## Output
The go/no-go assessment, the goal calculation table, the tier structure, and the timeline.
