# Brief

A 30-40 second animated short, in English, for a general audience, about the Great Molasses Flood
(Boston, January 15, 1919): a giant storage tank burst and a wave of molasses swept through the North End.

- 5 to 8 scenes, one sentence each, true facts only.
- Open on the hook with a punchy camera move.
- Captions only (no voice).
